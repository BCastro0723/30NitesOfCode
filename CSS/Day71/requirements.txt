Time build a Pokémon card collection, 
    the kind you'd want to show off to your friends! 🃏

You'll start with a set of Pokémon cards that 
    already have a flip animation built in!

Click any card to flip it over and reveal the Pokémon. 
    Your job is to use flexbox to lay them out correctly.

In the styles.css file, use what you've learned to 
    arrange the cards. Things to think about include:

    - Use display: flex and flex-wrap on .card-collection 
      so cards wrap onto new lines when there isn't 
      enough room.
    - Using justify-content to space the cards evenly 
      across each row.
    - Using gap to add breathing room between the cards.

The flip animation is already handled for you! 
    Focus purely on the layout! And if you resize the 
    preview window, see how your cards gracefully 
    collapse into multiple columns! ♥️

Don't forget to share a screenshot of your finished collection on Twitter and tag @codedex_io!
