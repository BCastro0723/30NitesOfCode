Mid Drift

Favorite Album: Good At Avoiding

Bursting with sun-soaked melodies and woozy guitar licks, indie-rock sweethearts Mid Drift have swiftly captured hearts all across Australia selling out TWO national headliner tours in 2025. Between major support slots alongside Aussie crowd favourites Ocean Alley, Old Mervs, and DICE and appearances at renowned Australian festivals such as Laneway Festival and Rolling Sets Festival, the female fronted 5-piece show no signs of slowing down having just released their sophomore EP in February 2026.
